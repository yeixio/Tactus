# Privacy policy for the Tactus apps

_Last updated: November 25, 2026_

This policy covers Tactus for desktop and the Tactus apps for iPhone, iPad and Android, published by YEIXIO LLC.

## What Tactus collects

Nothing. Tactus has no account, and it does not collect, store or send any personal data, usage data or device identifiers. It has no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting.

## What stays on your device

The songs and sketches you make are saved on your own device, in the app's storage or in the folders you choose. They leave your device only when you share them yourself, for example by exporting audio or sending a project file through your phone's share sheet, and then only to the place you choose.

## Network access

The apps work without a network connection. They do not contact any server run by YEIXIO LLC or anyone else.

## MIDI and audio

The desktop app can use MIDI controllers you connect. Tactus uses them only to play and control music while it runs, and records nothing from them except mappings you save in your project.

## Children

Tactus collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/yeixio/Tactus/issues.
