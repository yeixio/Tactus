The first release of Tactus: the whole creative loop works end to end.

- **Studio:** clips and scenes, a piano roll that knows your key, a drum step sequencer, a timeline and a mixer.
- **Code:** the Tactus language with live updates on the next beat or bar. A mistake never stops the music.
- **Instrument Lab:** smart controls up front, with the full synth behind them.
- **Perform:** scene launchers, faders, performance macros, MIDI learn and recording to WAV.
- **Inspiration engine:** generators for drums, basslines, chords, arpeggios, melodies and variations.
- **Engine:** real-time audio on its own thread, a subtractive and FM synth, an 8-pad drum sampler with four kits, effects and a master bus.
- **Projects** are folders of readable files, with autosave and undo. Export to WAV at 16 or 24-bit.

### Install

Download the installer for your system below.

- **macOS:** the `.dmg`. The app isn't signed yet, so right-click it and choose **Open** the first time.
- **Windows:** the `.exe` installer. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning because the app isn't signed yet.
- **Linux:** the `.AppImage`. Make it executable, then run it.
