# Factory sound credits

Every recording in this folder is in the public domain or released under CC0 1.0, so it can be used, changed and sold freely, including in the paid Tactus apps. No attribution is required, but we credit the people who made them anyway.

The files were trimmed, normalized and re-encoded as FLAC by `scripts/build-sound-pack.py`.

## Piano (`piano/`)

From [Splendid Grand Piano](https://github.com/sfzinstruments/SplendidGrandPiano): a Steinway grand that Akai released into the public domain, cleaned up by the sfzinstruments project. Tactus uses the mezzo-forte layer, one recording every three semitones, named by MIDI note number.
